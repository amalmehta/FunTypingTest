How WPM is calculated

By convention a “word” is 5 characters, spaces included (Wikipedia: Words per minute). So WPM counts characters, not actual words, which keeps short and long words fair.

1. Speed (the big number)

WPM=Cs/5t/60
  • Cs — scored characters: every letter of each word you typed exactly right, plus one space after it, plus the word you’re on if it’s right so far
  • t — time since your first keystroke, in seconds

Only correct words count, so mistakes you leave behind cost you speed.

2. Raw speed

Raw=Ct/5t/60
  • Ct — every character you typed and kept, right or wrong, including spaces

How fast your fingers moved, ignoring mistakes.

3. Accuracy

Accuracy=KcKc+Ki×100%
  • Kc — correct keystrokes (a space counts as correct when the word before it is right)
  • Ki — incorrect keystrokes, even ones you later fixed with Backspace

The share of keys you hit right the first time.

The raw-speed and accuracy rules are this app’s conventions; typing sites differ in the details. The ghost pacer assumes you typed your best run at an even pace.
