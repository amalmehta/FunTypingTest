How WPM is calculated

By convention a “word” is 5 characters, spaces included (Wikipedia: Words per minute). So WPM counts characters, not actual words, which keeps short and long words fair.

1. Raw (gross) WPM

Gross=Ct/5m
  • Ct — every character in what you typed, right or wrong, including spaces
  • m — minutes since your first keystroke

How fast your fingers moved, ignoring mistakes.

2. Net WPM (the big number)

Net=max(0,Gross−Em)
  • E — uncorrected errors: wrong, extra and skipped letters still in the text at the end

Each mistake you leave behind costs one word per minute. Mistakes you fix cost only the time it took.

3. Accuracy

Accuracy=KcKt×100%
  • Kc — correct keystrokes (a space counts as correct when the word before it is right)
  • Kt — all keystrokes except Backspace, so a mistake counts even if you later fix it

The share of keys you hit right the first time.

These are the standard gross/net WPM and accuracy definitions. The ghost pacer assumes you typed your best run at an even pace.
